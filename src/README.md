## Smart Leftover Optimizer
An LLM-Driven Daily Meal Repurposing Assistant for Household Food Waste Minimization

## Problem statement
Busy households, students, and home cooks who must deal with daily meal planning routinely throw away edible, pre-cooked leftovers as they do not know how to immediately transform them into recipes that match the exact ingredient decay windows. This inefficiency because excessive household organic waste directly increases family living costs and significantly contributes to landfill accumulation and preventable greenhouse gas emissions. Households are the biggest contributor to food waste in Malaysia, with an estimated 17,000 tons of food wasted daily, according to the Solid Waste and Public Cleansing Management Corporation (SWCorp). Of this, more than 24% is edible leftover food that can be consumed or repurposed.
