# User Stories

Format: As a [type of user], I want [goal], so that [reason or benefit].

1. As a busy student with limited cooking time, I want to generate quick recipes using my near-expiry cooked rice and leftover chicken, so that I can prepare a fresh dinner in under 20 minutes without wasting food. (FR1,FR3,FR5)
Acceptance Criteria 1: "Cooked Rice 1 day left" and "cooked chicken," prioritization of these two ingredients in the recommended recipe compared to other items stored longer in the pantry. 
Acceptance Criteria 2: The application shows preparation and cooking time that should not exceed 20 minutes.

2. As a health-conscious home cook, I want the system to warn me if I input a leftover item that is no longer safe to consume, so that I do not accidentally prepare unsafe food. (FR2)
Acceptance Criteria 1: Entering a high-risk food item with a shelf life threshold greater than 4 days such as "cooked seafood, 3 days old" displays a visual warning banner on screen. 
Acceptance Criteria 2: Unsafe foods are filtered out from the LLM prompt to prevent generating recipes for expired foods.

3. As a budget-focused household manager, I want recipe suggestions that rely strictly on what is in my fridge and basic pantry staples, so that I do not have to buy extra specialty ingredients at the grocery store. (FR4,FR6)
Acceptance Criteria 1: The ingredients used for the recipe generated consist solely of the products chosen from the user input of inventory and verified pantry items example oil, salt, soy sauce. 
Acceptance Criteria 2: For the non-pantry item to be included in the recipe generated by the LLM, it will have to be separately noted as optional garnishes.
